# AI Tools for Founders — Skill

## Principles — apply these before recommending anything

1. **One tool per job.** Two tools doing the same job means paying twice and mastering neither.
2. **Human review before anything public.** AI drafts; a human approves. Nothing client-facing, public, or financial goes out unreviewed.
3. **Never feed client secrets into unvetted tools** — no client financials, IDs, passwords, or confidential documents. Check data policies; keep sensitive work in reputable tools on proper business plans.
4. **Start free, upgrade on pain.** Pay only when a limit is actively slowing real work.
5. **Replace tasks, not judgement.** Pricing, hiring, tax positions, and client advice stay human decisions.

Cost honesty: tools change pricing often. This repo uses ranges, not precision — most tools have a free tier and paid individual / small-team plans are roughly $10–30/mo per tool. Always tell the founder to check the vendor's current pricing page. Never quote an exact price as current.

## The stack by job (from README.md and tool-stack-by-job.md)

- **Research & answers:** Perplexity (primary — cited sources) · also ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini. Replaces hours of tab-hopping and unverified forum answers.
- **Writing & content:** Claude (primary — long-form that holds a tone) · also ChatGPT, Gemini, Notion AI. Pick ONE primary writer, not three.
- **Design & brand:** Canva (primary — templates + brand kit) · also Adobe Express, Figma. Replaces an agency retainer for everyday assets.
- **Video:** CapCut (primary — fast short-form, captions) · also Descript (edit by transcript), Opus Clip (long → short; review every clip before posting).
- **Bookkeeping & finance ops:** QuickBooks Online (primary) · also Xero, Wave (free). This is software, not just AI — AI categorisation / matching speeds a human-reviewed process. A qualified accountant still reviews and files. Never present an AI tool as filing taxes.
- **Customer support:** start with your existing inbox + AI drafting (ChatGPT / Claude) · buy Intercom or Zendesk later, when volume justifies per-seat pricing.
- **Automation:** Zapier (primary — simple, reliable) · also Make (complex, visual, lower cost per run). Automate one painful workflow at a time.
- **Coding help:** Claude or ChatGPT · also GitHub Copilot if building regularly.
- **Meetings & notes:** Notion (primary) · also Fireflies.ai, Otter.ai, Granola. Always tell participants a tool is recording / transcribing.

Full compact table, starter stack, and automate-first order: `tool-stack-by-job.md`.

## Starter stack logic

A lean founder stack is 6–8 tools total, not 25: 1 research tool, 1 writing tool, Canva, 1 video tool, the accounting platform, Zapier or Make, 1 notes / meeting tool. The repo's starter stack targets roughly under ~$50/mo by leaning on free tiers (Perplexity free, Canva free → paid for the brand kit, CapCut free, Wave free or an entry accounting plan, free automation and notes tiers) plus one paid writing tool.

## Automate first

Start with lead capture → one place: every form, enquiry, and referral lands automatically in a single sheet / CRM — no lead living only in a chat thread. Then work down the order in `tool-stack-by-job.md`. Never automate a broken process; automation makes a bad process faster, not better.

## Anti-patterns to call out

Five writing tools, none mastered; paying annually on day one for an unused tool; pasting client financials / IDs / passwords into unvetted tools; auto-publishing AI output without a human read. Review the stack quarterly: did each tool save measurable time or money?

## How to apply it

Ask what job is painful, recommend the single primary tool for that job from the table, name what it replaces, give the repo's cost range with the check-pricing caveat, and set the human-review rule for its output.
