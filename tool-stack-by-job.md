# Tool Stack by Job — Compact Reference

The whole stack on one page. One tool per job. Free tiers first, paid plans are roughly **$10–30/mo per tool** unless noted — always confirm current pricing with the vendor.

## Starter Stack — Under ~$50/mo

For a founder starting from zero. Built to stay modest: free tiers do the heavy lifting, and only two paid subscriptions are essential early.

| Slot | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Research | Perplexity (free) | Cited answers without a subscription |
| Writing | Claude **or** ChatGPT (paid, ~$20/mo class) — pick ONE | This is the engine: posts, emails, scripts, analysis |
| Design | Canva (free → paid ~$10–15/mo class when you need the brand kit) | Every graphic, deck, and carousel |
| Video | CapCut (free) | Reels and shorts with captions |
| Bookkeeping | Wave (free) or QuickBooks/Xero entry plan if you're already trading | Clean books from day one |
| Automation | Zapier or Make (free tier) | Connect forms, sheets, and email |
| Notes | Notion (free) | Docs, meeting notes, content bank |

**Typical total: roughly $20–50/mo** depending on which tiers you upgrade — versus a VA or agency retainer for the same output. Upgrade a tier only when its limit blocks real work, not because a launch video told you to.

## What to Automate First

In this order — each step pays for the next:

1. **Lead capture → one place.** Every form, DM enquiry, and referral lands in a single sheet/CRM automatically (Zapier/Make). No lead ever lives only in a chat thread.
2. **Meeting notes.** Transcribe and summarise every client call. Decisions and action items stop depending on memory.
3. **Content drafting pipeline.** Idea bank → AI first draft → human edit → publish at human pace. Automate the drafting, never the publishing judgement.
4. **Bookkeeping categorisation.** Bank feeds + AI categorisation in QuickBooks/Xero, human-reviewed weekly. Month-end stops being a rescue mission.
5. **Support drafts.** AI drafts replies to your top 10 recurring questions; a human reviews and sends. Response time drops, quality stays yours.
6. **Follow-up reminders.** Automatic nudges for unpaid invoices, pending documents, and stale leads — the work everyone forgets and nobody should do by hand.

## Anti-Patterns

- ❌ Five AI writing tools, none mastered
- ❌ Paying annually on day one ("to save 20%") for a tool you haven't used for a month
- ❌ Pasting client financials, IDs, or passwords into unvetted tools
- ❌ Auto-publishing AI output — social posts, emails, or support replies — without a human read
- ❌ Automating a broken process (automation makes a bad process faster, not better)

## Review Cadence

Once a quarter, for every paid tool, answer three questions:

1. Did it save measurable time or money this quarter?
2. Is there a free tier or an existing tool that now does this job?
3. Would I buy it again today at this price?

Two "no" answers = cancel it. Stacks should shrink as often as they grow.
